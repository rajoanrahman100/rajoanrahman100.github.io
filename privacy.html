Privacy Policy — Steady Set: 12 Weeks PPL

Effective date: October 18, 2026

This policy explains how the Steady Set: 12 Weeks PPL app ("Steady Set", "the app") handles your information. The app is published by rtp99 LLC ("we", "us").

The short version: your training and body data stays on your phone. There is no account and we run no servers, so we never receive your workouts, measurements or photos. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, which are not personalised and are described below.

1. Information the app stores on your device

To run your 12-week program, the app saves the following only on your phone, in the app's private storage:

  • Your plan and training: training days, plan choice, workouts, sets, weights, reps, rest settings and exercise swaps.
  • Your name, if you choose to enter one, used only to greet you in the app.
  • Your daily logs: the steps and protein you enter for each day, used to fill in your check-ins.
  • Your body details: sex (used only to pick the calorie formula), age, height, goal weight, weigh-ins, waist and hip measurements, and check-in answers, including whether you reported dizziness, ongoing fatigue or missed periods.
  • Progress photos, if you take or add any.
  • Your settings: units, reminder times, rest-timer and countdown preferences, and ad-free day and ad display timing.

We do not have access to any of this. It is not uploaded to us or to anyone else by the app. Some of it is health-related; it is used only to show your plan, targets and progress inside the app, and it is never used for advertising.

2. Information we do not collect

  • No account, email address or phone number. (A name, if you enter one, stays on your phone; see Section 1.)
  • No location.
  • No contacts.
  • No analytics or tracking of how you use the app.
  • No sale or sharing of your information.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob: banners on some screens, an occasional full-screen ad after a workout, and an optional video that removes ads for a day.

  • Ads are non-personalised. The app asks Google for ads that are not based on your past activity, and it does not ask for Apple's permission to track you across apps (so the Identifier for Advertisers, IDFA, is not available to the app).
  • What Google may still collect: to deliver and measure ads, limit how often you see them, and prevent fraud, Google's ads software may collect information such as your IP address, device and operating-system details, an app-specific device identifier, ad interactions (such as views and taps) and diagnostic information. Google processes this under its own policies:
    • How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
    • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • No health data is sent to Google. The app does not pass your workouts, measurements, photos or any other information from Section 1 to the ads service.
  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): where the law requires it, the app shows Google's consent form before any ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Profile → Privacy and ads (shown where this applies).

4. Fonts

The app uses Google Fonts. The first time the app opens, it may download its typefaces from Google's servers, which means Google receives your IP address and basic request information. No personal data from the app is included. See https://developers.google.com/fonts/faq/privacy.

5. Permissions the app may ask for

  • Camera and photo library: only if you choose to take or add progress photos. Photos are copied into the app's private storage and are not saved to your camera roll or shared.
  • Notifications: only if you turn on the rest-timer alerts or reminders (morning weigh-in, check-in due). These notifications are created on your phone; we do not send them from a server.

You can turn any of these off in your phone's settings at any time.

6. Backups you create

The app can create a backup file of your data, with or without photos, when you tap Back up now. The file is handed to your phone's share sheet, and you choose where it goes, for example Files, iCloud Drive, Google Drive or a message to yourself. Once saved there, it is handled under that service's privacy terms. We never receive your backup files. You can restore a backup only by picking the file yourself.

Your phone's own system backups (such as iCloud Backup or Android backup) may also include the app's data, depending on your phone's settings. These are managed by Apple or Google, not by us.

7. Keeping and deleting your data

  • Your data stays on your phone until you delete it.
  • You can delete individual weigh-ins and photos in the app, and clear a day's steps or protein by setting it to 0.
  • Deleting the app removes all of its data from your phone. Copies remain only in backup files you saved yourself or in your phone's system backups.

Because we do not hold your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. For information Google may hold about ads, see Google's controls at https://myaccount.google.com.

8. Security

Your data is protected by your phone's own security (passcode, Face ID or Touch ID, fingerprint, and encryption of app storage). Keep your phone and any backup files you save secure.

9. Not medical advice

Steady Set is a fitness and training tool. Calorie, protein and progress information is general guidance, not medical advice. Talk to a doctor before starting if you have a medical condition, are pregnant or postpartum, or have a history of eating disorders.

10. Age

The app is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EEA and UK, or under California and other US state laws), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the use of your personal information.

  • Your training and body data is under your control on your phone: you can view, correct and delete it in the app, or delete the app.
  • For ad-related information, use Profile → Privacy and ads in the app (where shown) and Google's tools linked in Section 3.
  • We do not sell your personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and, for significant changes, mention it in the app's release notes.
