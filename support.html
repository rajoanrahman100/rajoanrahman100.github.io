Moving to a new phone

In the app, open Profile → Backup → Back up now and save the file somewhere you can reach from the new phone, such as iCloud Drive. On the new phone, install Steady Set and choose Have a backup? Restore it on the first screen.

The rest timer is quiet or silent

The end-of-rest chime follows the iPhone silent switch. Check the switch and your volume, and turn on Sound under Profile → Workout timer. To see the timer on your Lock Screen, allow notifications and Live Activities for Steady Set in iPhone Settings.

Ads

Steady Set is free and shows non-personalised ads, never during a workout. Watch one short video under Profile → Ads to turn ads off for 24 hours.

Deleting your data

Everything is stored only on your phone. Deleting the app removes all of it.
